SOM AL MACBA

MOTION AND ADVERTISING, 2024

Creative Direction, Motion & Design

Mariana Esteves

Context

Developed at BAU Barcelona

Fictious campaign for MACBA, exploring its relationship with the city of Barcelona, its culture, and its profound Catalan identity.

MACBA's energy manifests itself both in and outside its doors — this campaign aims to capture the fast-paced enthusiasm bustling in the Plaça dels Àngels, rather than focusing on the artworks indoors. Presenting MACBA as the face of a new, transformed energy in the centre of the Raval neighborhood, touching on all the different agents that make up this energy was essential; bringing an "underground" culture to the forefront through movement, sound and rythm.

A proposal for outdoor and social media application is then suggested, expanding the identity created for the video.
