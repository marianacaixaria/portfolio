Context

Developed at the Faculty of Fine Arts, University of Lisbon

Paredes de Coura sits in the Alto Minho, in a valley of granite hills and woodland where the river Coura runs down toward the Minho. Often overlooked, it is a place covered with the unmistakable warmth of the North. This academic identity project embraces that character, drawing on the textures, sounds, people and natural elements that define the region.

The visual and verbal language had to feel rooted in the land and in the lives of the people who live there. Texture played a big part in bringing the identity to life, both in the typography and in the illustrations, which draw on natural elements such as the river Taboão and the stone-paved streets. Those vectorized illustrations also work as textures in their own right, layering over the visual system as overlays that enrich it. Above all, the tone aims to represent a place that doesn’t try to be more than it is, and that is its strength.

The result is an identity that moves confidently in harmony with the village it represents. Coura is just Coura, and nothing else.
